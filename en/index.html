About me

My name is Manuel Canales Quintanilla. As a graphic designer, I do not just practice a profession; I also live my passion for design and creativity. In my free time, photography is my escape and my form of expression. I always strive to make a positive contribution and work toward a better Peru. I sincerely value simple, kind-hearted people, because they are the true essence of our society.

Frequently asked questions

⏱️

What are the delivery timelines?

They depend on the complexity of the project.

💳

How are payments made?

50% upfront and 50% upon completion.

🏦

What payment methods do you accept?

BCP, Yape/Plin, PayPal, and PagoEfectivo.

📦

What is the delivery method?

Google Drive or WeTransfer.

Delivery timelines

Delivery timelines will vary depending on the complexity of the project. We will be happy to agree on them specifically after reviewing the service request and determining the actions to be developed.

Payments

Once an agreement is reached, a 50% payment of the total agreed amount will be required as an advance. The remaining 50% will be paid upon completion of the final proposal presentation in order to proceed with the release of the files. Please note that editable files may involve an additional cost. At the end of the service, a professional services receipt will be issued.

Payment methods

We accept different payment methods for your convenience. You can make bank deposits through Banco de Crédito BCP, transfer via Yape or Plin, use PayPal in U.S. dollars with additional fees, choose Pago Link with a surcharge, or use a PagoEfectivo CIP code with an additional surcharge. We recommend choosing the option that works best for you.

Delivery

For file delivery, we offer two main options: a shared Google Drive link where you can access and download the files directly, or a transfer through WeTransfer. Please note that the WeTransfer link expires 3 days after it is sent. If you prefer a different delivery method or have a specific request, we can coordinate it beforehand to meet your needs.
